---
name: Equifax subscriber address
description: Which address belongs on a landlord's Equifax Broker Subscriber Agreement.
---

The Equifax Broker Subscriber Agreement must use an address supplied by the landlord for their business/subscriber, not an address inferred from a rental property. A business name collected in onboarding does not establish the business address. Ask the landlord to provide and confirm the address if it has not been collected explicitly.

**Why:** The user stated rental property and business addresses differ almost all the time, and rejected assuming one from the other.

**How to apply:** In any agreement-generation or re-signing flow, use only an expressly supplied subscriber/business address. Do not use a property's address as a fallback.
