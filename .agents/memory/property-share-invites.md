---
name: Property share invite intent
description: The distinction between a property link's starting destination and the scope of a landlord invite.
---

Property-specific share links identify the listing that prompted the invitation, but they do **not** restrict tenants to that single listing. Acceptance starts a 14-day period limited to all active listings of the inviting landlord. The property link persists until that property is deleted; deleting it prevents new redemptions without cancelling existing tenant windows. Existing accepted windows are not retroactively extended.

**Why:** The owner explicitly clarified that the older 10-day landlord-wide inventory rule should remain, changing only its duration to 14 days, alongside long-lived property links.

**How to apply:** Keep property destination/navigation separate from listing visibility and application eligibility. Do not make a property token a one-unit permission or reset an active restriction by clicking a link again.
